Privacy Policy for Unbound Services

**Last Updated: October 9, 2026**

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Unbound Team ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our private game server and its launcher for Home Sweet Home: Online (the "Service").

By using our Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information
- Steam ID and Steam Username (collected via Steam authentication)
- Discord ID (the unique identifier for your Discord account)

### Gameplay Data
- In-game statistics and progress
- Character data and game settings
- Game session information

### Technical and Security Information

The Home Sweet Home: Online game client automatically transmits certain technical information to our server, including:
- Mac addresses (sent by the game client for security and anti-cheat purposes)
- Game client version
- Connection logs and error reports
- Server performance data

**Important Note:** Mac addresses are collected because the game client sends this information to the server as part of its normal operation. We use this data solely for security, authentication, and preventing abuse of our Service.

## How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:
- Provide and maintain the game service
- Manage user accounts using Steam IDs and Discord IDs, and authenticate users via Steam
- Monitor and improve server performance
- Prevent cheating, hacking, abuse, and security threats
- Detect and prevent ban evasion
- Enforce our Terms of Service and community rules
- Investigate violations and respond to player reports

## Data Storage and Security

- **Primary Database:** Essential gameplay data (statistics, progress, character data) is stored in MongoDB servers located in Singapore regions.
- **Security Data:** Mac addresses are stored separately from gameplay data and are retained for a maximum of 90 days for security and anti-cheat purposes only
- **Data Encryption:** We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information
- **Access Control:** Access to personal data is restricted to authorized team members only
- **Automatic Deletion:** Security data (Mac addresses) is automatically purged after 90 days unless required for ongoing investigations

## Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:
- With your explicit consent
- To comply with legal obligations or valid legal requests from law enforcement
- To protect the rights, property, or safety of our Service, users, or others
- With Steam/Valve Corporation as required for authentication purposes

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access:** Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you (including Steam IDs, Discord IDs, gameplay data, collected Mac addresses)
- **Deletion:** Request deletion of your account and associated data
- **Correction:** Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Restriction:** Request restriction of certain data processing (note: some features may require specific data to function properly; restricting essential data may limit your ability to use the Service)

To exercise these rights, contact us at **hshunbound.team@gmail.com**

**Response Time:** We will respond to data requests within 10 days.

## Data Retention

- **Account Information (Steam ID, Steam Username, and Discord ID):** Retained as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, subject to the account deletion provisions below
- **Gameplay Data:** Retained as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service
- **Security Data (Mac Addresses):** Automatically deleted after 90 days, except where retention is necessary for ongoing investigations or to comply with legal obligations
- **Account Deletion:** You may request account deletion at any time. Upon request, we will delete or anonymize your data within 7-10 business days, except where we must retain certain information to comply with legal obligations or complete ongoing investigations
